About Me

profile.md

I am a technology student and software builder with a background in Systems and Computer Programming, currently pursuing my degree in Information Systems and Technologies (STI) at NOVA IMS. I am driven by a deep curiosity about how systems work under the hood, with long-term ambitions in Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence.

Technologically, I enjoy exploring beyond abstractions, working across Python, Linux environments, systems programming, and hardware-software integration. I have a strong builder-oriented mindset: rather than stopping at theory, I focus on turning concepts into modular, functioning tools, from autonomous AI agents and automated workflows to custom computing setups and robotics.

Academically and personally, I prioritize continuous optimization and practical impact. Outside of software engineering, I practice Fencing, learn piano, and explore how accessible technology can solve real-world challenges across European and African contexts.
